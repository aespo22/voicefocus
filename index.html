FAQ

Does my audio leave my phone?
No. Transcription, speaker separation and summaries run on device. If you turn on "Allow Private Cloud", Apple Intelligence may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute for summaries; we never receive that data.

Why is the first transcription slow?
On first use iOS may download its on-device speech model for your language, and VoiceFocus downloads its speaker-separation model. This happens once.

How do I delete my data?
Delete a conversation in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
