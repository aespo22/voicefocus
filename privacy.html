Privacy Policy

VoiceFocus · Last updated October 13, 2026

Summary

VoiceFocus does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. We have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Microphone and speech

VoiceFocus uses the microphone to transcribe conversations in real time. Speech recognition uses Apple's on-device speech frameworks. Audio and transcripts are processed and stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Transcripts, summaries and, if you enable "Save session audio", audio recordings are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. You can delete any conversation in the app at any time; deleting the app removes all of its data.

Summaries

Summaries are generated with Apple Intelligence on device. If you enable "Allow Private Cloud", Apple may process requests through Private Cloud Compute under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive this data.

Downloads

On first use the app may download speech and speaker-separation models. These downloads contain no personal data.

Children

VoiceFocus does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
